Definitions & limits

Separate source records. No arithmetic, interpolated history or automatic joins are inferred.

Four selected Mexican urban centres, 1975–2025, at five-year checkpoints already retained by Chronicle. Not all cities or annual values. Original values are estimates in thousands, converted to rounded persons; no uncertainty interval is supplied. Footprints are not established as constant. No interpolation, pooled totals, or growth rates. Other historical-source series and future projections are excluded.
