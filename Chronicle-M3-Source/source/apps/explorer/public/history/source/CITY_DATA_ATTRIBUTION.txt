Modern urban population data
---------------------------
United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division (2025). World Urbanization Prospects: The 2025 Revision, Online Edition. File 21 (POP/DB/WUP/Rev.2025/F21).
Copyright © 2025 United Nations. Made available under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO (CC BY 3.0 IGO): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/
https://population.un.org/wup/assets/Download/Cities/WUP2025-F21-DEGURBA-Cities_Pop.xlsx

Changes in this product: filtering, numerical unit conversion, astronomical year conversion, checkpoint sampling, conservative identity alignment, dated-name curation, and quarantine of identified suspect records. Historical estimates have different spatial definitions from the UN DEGURBA urban-centre series. Estimates through 2025 only; UN future projections excluded. No endorsement by the source authors or the United Nations is implied. Full source files, metadata, source hashes, and change receipts accompany the source distribution.
