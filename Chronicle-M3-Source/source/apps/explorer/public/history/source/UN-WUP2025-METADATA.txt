Data are presented  in the following datasheet for Cities as defined by the Degree of Urbanization using a combination of population size, density, and contiguity thresholds based on 1_x001E_km² population grids and satellite data to provide a consistent measure of urbanization across countries.
The following set of criteria is used to defined cities:
Cities (densely populated areas): These are areas with a high density (at least 1,500 inhabitants per km²) and a large population (at least 50,000 inhabitants).
The following column names describe each dataset:

PWCent_Longitude | Population-weighted centroid location for the city: longitude coordinate in decimal degrees
PWCent_Latitude | Population-weighted centroid location for the city: latitude coordinate in decimal degrees
